About Me

I am David Arturo Álvarez Suárez, a Sound Designer and Game Developer based in Bogotá, Colombia. I bridge the gap between a game's code and its soundtrack, combining formal musical training at UNAD with hands-on technical game development.

I thrive at the intersection of art and logic. Whether I am crafting audio textures in REAPER, wiring dynamic sound systems in Unity with FMOD, or scripting in C#, I know exactly how audio, visuals, and code must sync to create a fully immersive experience.
